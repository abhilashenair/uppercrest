About Upper Crest

A private homestay built on the belief that every traveller deserves the comfort of home — wherever they are.

Our Story

Where Comfort Meets Convenience

Upper Crest was created with a simple purpose — to offer travellers arriving at or departing from Cochin International Airport a truly comfortable, private and well-equipped place to stay.

Located in the quiet neighbourhood of Poikattusserry, Aluva, just 6.5 km from the airport, Upper Crest fills a gap that many travellers face: finding a clean, fully furnished, private space near the airport that feels like home rather than a transit room.

Whether you are a family visiting from abroad, a patient visiting a nearby hospital, or a business traveller needing a private base near the airport — Upper Crest is designed to serve your needs with warmth and care.

The Property

A Premium Furnished Homestay

Upper Crest is a standalone residential property in a peaceful residential area of Aluva, Kerala. The property includes a spacious furnished bedroom with an attached bathroom, a well-equipped kitchen, a comfortable living room and a dining area.

The property is equipped with air conditioning, power backup, washing machine and high-quality kitchen appliances — everything you need for a short or extended stay.
