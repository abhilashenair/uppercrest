Frequently Asked Questions

Useful information for guests searching for a homestay near Kochi Airport.

Is Upper Crest a homestay near Kochi Airport?

Yes. Upper Crest is located about 6.5 km from Cochin International Airport (COK), making it ideal for airport stayovers, families, married couples and travellers. It is one of the closest private furnished homestays to the airport.

Is Upper Crest suitable for long-term stays?

Yes. Upper Crest is suitable for both short-term and long-term stays with furnished rooms, kitchen facilities, living room, washing machine and power backup. Monthly rates are available — WhatsApp us for details.

Is food available at Upper Crest?

Breakfast, lunch and dinner can be arranged on request at an additional cost. Groceries and frozen foods can also be arranged on request.

Is Upper Crest near Ayurveda hospitals or wellness centres?

Yes. Upper Crest is located about 1.5 km from Badarinath Ayurveda, making it very convenient for Ayurveda treatment visitors and wellness travellers.

What amenities are provided at Upper Crest?

Stay amenities include furnished bedroom, attached bathroom, living room with TV, AC, dining area, kitchen with air fryer, induction cooker, LPG stove, refrigerator, washing machine and power backup. Guest toiletries include shaving kit, shower gel, shampoo, soap, dental kit and tea/coffee sachets.

What is the check-in and check-out time?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is before 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out can be arranged on request, subject to availability.

How far is Upper Crest from Cochin International Airport?

Upper Crest Homestay is approximately 6.5 km from Cochin International Airport (COK), which is about a 12–15 minute drive depending on traffic.

What is the address of Upper Crest Homestay?

Is this a private homestay or shared accommodation?

Upper Crest is completely private. There is only 1 unit — when you book, the entire property is exclusively yours with no other guests sharing any space.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Cash, UPI and Bank Transfer. Booking is confirmed via WhatsApp — no advance payment portal required.

Do guests need to complete check-in registration forms?

Yes. All guests should complete the relevant guest check-in form before or during check-in. Indian guests should use the Indian Guest form, and foreign passport holders should use the Foreign Guest form. The Guest Check-in page is linked from the footer and mobile check-in shortcut.

Is guest registration required for foreign nationals?

Yes. Foreign passport holders, including minors, must submit guest details as required for host records and FRRO-related compliance. Please use the Foreign Guest form on the Guest Check-in page.

Are smoking, pets, parties or events allowed?

Smoking is strictly prohibited inside the property. Pets are not allowed. Parties, gatherings and events are not permitted without prior approval from the host.

What are the quiet hours?

Quiet hours are from 11:30 PM to 5:30 AM. Guests are requested to keep noise levels low and respect the neighbours. If you expect visitors, kindly inform us in advance.

What should guests do before check-out?

Before check-out, please turn off lights, fans, AC and appliances, lock doors and windows, place used towels in the laundry basket, wash used dishes, dispose of waste in the designated bins and leave the apartment clean and tidy.

How should waste be disposed of?

Please separate food waste and biodegradable items into the Green Bin, and plastic, metal and other non-biodegradable waste into the Blue Bin. Please do not flush diapers, sanitary pads, wipes or other non-flushable items down the toilet.

Is parking available at the property?

Yes. Free parking is available inside the property. Please park only in the designated parking area and ensure the entrance and neighbouring properties are not blocked.

Is power backup available during power outages?

Yes. Upper Crest Homestay has a solar power backup system. During power outages, backup power is available for approximately 3 to 4 hours and supports essential lighting, ceiling fans and one air conditioner.

How should the air conditioner be used?

For comfort and energy efficiency, set the AC temperature between 24°C and 25°C, keep doors and windows closed while it is running, and turn it off when leaving the apartment.

Is Wi-Fi available?

Yes. The Wi-Fi name and password are provided during check-in.

Are security cameras installed?

Outdoor security cameras are installed for guest and property safety. Please do not tamper with or obstruct them.
